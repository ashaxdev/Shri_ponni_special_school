Together We Make a Difference in Transforming Lives

Shri Ponni Special School & Therapy Center is a dedicated institution focused on empowering children with developmental challenges. We believe that every child deserves access to quality education, therapy, and emotional support.

Our Story

We combine education + therapy + care to create a holistic development environment where every child can reach their full potential.

Our approach is rooted in compassion, backed by expertise, and driven by the belief that with the right support, every child can thrive. We work closely with families to create individualized plans that address each child's unique needs and strengths.

As a government-registered institution covered under CMCHIS, we ensure that quality education and therapy services are accessible to all families, regardless of their financial situation. Shri Ponni Trust stands as the foundation of our mission, enabling us to provide these essential services with unwavering dedication to every child we serve.
