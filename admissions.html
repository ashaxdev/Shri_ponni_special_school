Eligibility Criteria

We welcome children with the following conditions

Autism Spectrum Disorder Children diagnosed with autism who need specialized education and therapy support.
ADHD Children with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder requiring structured learning environments.
Learning Disabilities Children facing challenges in reading, writing, or mathematical skills.
Intellectual Disabilities Children with developmental delays requiring individualized educational approaches.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there any admission fee?

No, admission is completely free. We provide free education and therapy services up to 10th standard under the CMCHIS scheme.

What is the age range for admission?

We accept children from early intervention age (0–3 years) through secondary education (up to 10th standard).

Do you provide transportation?

Please contact us to discuss transportation options available in your area.

How long does the admission process take?

The process typically takes 1–2 weeks from initial consultation to enrollment, depending on assessment scheduling.
